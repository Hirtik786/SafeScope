# Code of Conduct

SafeScope follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and
remember that many contributors are beginners or are writing in their second language.
Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/Karim-Bazooka). All reports are reviewed and kept confidential.
